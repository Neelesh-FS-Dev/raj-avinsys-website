Raj Avinsys: Competitive Landscape and Strategic Benchmark

Subject: Raj Avinsys Pvt Ltd (formerly Raj Electricals), rajgrouponline.com · Prepared 23 Nov 2026 · Sources: live site scrapes, Firecrawl search, and the competitor list supplied by the client
Written for: Raj Avinsys leadership and sales, to plan website and business changes.
How to read this report. Every claim carries a status tag. VERIFIED = seen on the company's own website or a search result during this review. CLAIM = stated by the company or a supplied list, not checked against documents. NOT FOUND = could not be confirmed; do not use externally. ASSUMPTION = a working hypothesis to test with the sales team.

1. Executive summary

  • Raj Avinsys is a South Gujarat turnkey contractor. Its verified footprint is Dahej (23 of 106 portfolio entries), Ankleshwar (16), Panoli, Vapi and Surat. It covers HT/LT electrification, 66 kV switchyards (one 220 kV erection package), sandwich busways (51 entries) and in-house MCC/PCC panel manufacturing since 2022.
  • The main gaps are in engineering and proof, not in execution. Competitors show type-test or approval status, studies and testing services, named technical pages, and clear contact details. Raj Avinsys shows none of these in verifiable form on its site.
  • Trust leaks are fixable quickly. The placeholder phone numbers and unverified certification badges are the most damaging items for procurement teams.
  • Several items in the supplied analysis are not supported by evidence and must be corrected before use (section 7). Most important: the "incorporated March 2026" date conflicts with the company's own 2013 record, and "1,000+ industrial projects" has no source. The published portfolio has 106 entries.

5. Depth analysis: strengths, gaps and what to fix

5a. Structural strengths (verified from the portfolio)

  • Concentrated presence: 23 entries in Dahej, 16 in Ankleshwar, 6 in Vapi, 6 in Surat, 7 in Panoli. This is a real local base to build location pages on.
  • Repeat-work signal: 51 sandwich busway entries across 46 client names, including several repeat orders from the same client (for example Schott Glass, MG Motors, Asian Paints, Garden Silk Mills). Repeat business is the strongest evidence of quality.
  • Breadth: electrification (37), switchyards (15), consultancy (3), plus panels since 2022.
  • Retention: 73% client retention on maintenance (company-stated). This is strong if documented.

5c. Shared operational baseline

  • All regional peers work in the Gujarat industrial belt, pitch to plant heads and PMCs, and pass through the same statutory approvals (electrical contractor licence, CEIG, GETCO and DISCOM connections).
  • Revenue for all is project-led, with repeat and maintenance work on top. Tender prequalification matters for utility work.
  • Quality certification (ISO) and safety compliance are standard table stakes.

8. Open questions for the company (blocking items)

  1. Certificate of incorporation: which date is correct, 2013 or 2026?
  2. Electrical Contractor licence (Category A): number, issuing authority and expiry. Copy of GETCO approval and ISO certificate (number, body, scope, validity).
  3. Branch phone numbers and addresses for Vapi and Ankleshwar.
  4. Permission to name clients, with a list of clients who agree. Which logos may be used?
  5. Is a 220 kV EPC project confirmed for the company itself (not the L&T subcontract)? Which 66 kV project details can be published?
  6. Is there a standing team for studies, testing or AMC? If not, these should not be marketed.
  7. Is type-tested or OEM-authorised panel supply available or in progress? Does any MNC tender require it now?
  8. Turnover for the last audited year, and client count definition.
